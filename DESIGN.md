## Brand & Style

This design system embodies quiet luxury, architectural restraint, and raw tactile materiality tailored for high-specification European architectural surfaces and circular construction materials. 

The aesthetic synthesizes Swiss rationalism and Mediterranean mineral heritage. The visual environment mirrors an architect’s atelier or an editorial monograph: generous margins, measured balance, unhurried typography, and physical tactility. The interface recedes to foreground material specimens, acoustic properties, environmental certifications, and aggregate textures.

### Design Principles
- **Architectural Restraint**: Form follows materiality. Avoid decorative artifacts, overt gradients, or playful roundings. Precision aligns to crisp gridlines and structural hairline dividers.
- **Quiet Confidence**: Avoid high-intensity saturation and dramatic dropshadows. Visual hierarchy is established through typographic scale, calculated white space, and surface tone shifts.
- **Physicality and Authenticity**: Interfaces evoke stone, plaster, rammed earth, and oxidized minerals. Surfaces operate like cut stone slabs—flat, planar, and meticulously engineered.
- **Editorial Legibility**: Copy reads as an architectural journal. Headlines in refined serifs bring gravitas and craft, anchored by utilitarian grotesque sans-serifs for engineering data, technical specifications, and navigational clarity.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from geological and ecological elements: natural hydraulic lime, slaked plaster, deep volcanic basalt, dried terracotta clay, and highland eucalyptus.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base substrate is not bleached digital white (#FFFFFF). The primary canvas is warm lime plaster (`#FAF8F5`), shifting to natural slaked stone (`#F4F1EA`) for recessed sections or secondary layers, and raw limestone (`#E6E2DA`) for structural panels and active states.
- **Ink & Typography**: Deep charcoal obsidian (`#1C1D1B`) acts as primary ink, providing deliberate contrast without the harsh synthetic severity of pure black. Secondary body copy, technical labels, and metadata utilize slate gray (`#656763`) and weathered basalt (`#2B2D2A`).
- **Accents & Earth Tones**: 
  - *Primary Accent (Eucalyptus / Muted Forest)*: `#384637` (with hover state `#4A5849`). Evokes sustainable forestry, bio-composites, and low-carbon binding agents. Applied to primary calls to action, active specifications, and verified certifications.
  - *Secondary Accent (Terracotta / Fired Clay)*: `#B3634B` (with dark variant `#9E523C`). Used with extreme restraint (e.g., thermal performance alerts, acoustic ratings, key material origin tags).
- **Hairlines & Grid Structure**: Structural framework lines use hairline tints (`#E2DDD4` and `#D5CFBF`), representing mortar joints and architectural draft lines.

## Typography

The typography pairs an authoritative, high-craft editorial serif (`Newsreader`) with a calibrated geometric-grotesque (`DM Sans`).

### Editorial Serif Hierarchy
- Headlines and editorial narrative blocks use `Newsreader`.
- Text renders strictly in sentence case. Never set `Newsreader` in all-caps.
- Weights remain strictly at 400 (Regular) and 500 (Medium). Never use bold or heavy weights, preserving the delicate cut of transitional letterforms.

### Technical Grotesque Hierarchy
- All data tables, navigational nodes, interface controls, measurements (e.g., $kgCO_2e/m^2$, $\lambda$-values), and micro-copy utilize `DM Sans`.
- Labels and micro-metadata apply tracked small caps or slight positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) at 500 weight to ensure immediate scannability across engineering datasheets and blueprint schedules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model takes inspiration from classical architectural plate layouts and Miesian structural grids.

### Grid Dynamics
- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column proportional grid with generous `4rem` outer canvas margins and `2.5rem` gutters. Content containers enforce a maximum readable column width of 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Material matrices adjust from 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Hairline grid dividers collapse from vertical joints into horizontal divider tiers.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals and editorial pacing follow an incremental architectural scale:
- `space-xs` (4px): Fine detail alignment, badge padding, and icon-to-label gaps.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner paddings and compact table cellular gaps.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard element groupings, chip arrays, and card content gaps.
- `space-lg` (28px): Section sub-groupings and card interior perimeter padding.
- `space-xl` (48px): Structural breaks between material property modules and technical chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual plane differentiation avoids digital shadows, blur radiuses, or simulated lighting heights. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering**, **coplanar materiality**, and **structural hairlines**.

### Surface Tiering
- **Base Bed (Layer 0)**: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Plaster). The primary page canvas.
- **Monolithic Container (Layer 1)**: `#F4F1EA` (Slaked Limestone). Used for specification panels, technical drawers, and card grounds.
- **Recessed / Pressed State (Layer 2)**: `#E6E2DA` (Poured Stone). Inset panels, filter fields, and active selection tabs.

### Hairline Delimitation
Separation of components, columns, and data cells relies exclusively on a 1px solid hairline (`#E2DDD4` on canvas, `#D5CFBF` on cards). Boundaries simulate physical stone cut-lines and dry joints rather than depth shadows.

### Floating Planes
When modals, material sample drawers, or contextual menus must float, depth is rendered through a sharp 1px structural border (`#1C1D1B` at 15% opacity) accompanied by an ambient, broad, near-invisible shadow:
`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 29, 27, 0.06);`
No high-contrast elevation or saturated ambient glows are permitted.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a zero-radius geometry (`0px` / `rounded-none`), reflecting cut masonry, quarry blocks, structural steel, and planar architectural drawings.

### Form Execution
- All interactive controls, cards, input elements, modal surfaces, and spec sheets feature strictly orthogonal, 90-degree corners.
- Micro elements such as indicator dots (e.g., live batch availability) remain circular, serving as distinct focal points against the rectilinear architecture.
- Structural cutouts, progress bars, and swatch modules retain razor-sharp edge definition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background in deep eucalyptus (`#384637`), text in warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`), 0px radius, uppercase/tracked label font (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#4A5849`. Active state slightly indents via internal padding shift or deep tone `#2B362A`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background transparent, 1px perimeter border in `#1C1D1B`, text in `#1C1D1B`. Hover fills the background with raw limestone (`#E6E2DA`) without shifting borders.
- **Text Link**: Underlined with a 1px solid hairline offset by 4px in `#656763`, shifting to `#1C1D1B` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Unselected filter chips use a 1px border of `#E2DDD4` on transparent background with `#656763` text.
- Selected chips shift to a solid `#1C1D1B` fill with `#FAF8F5` text.
- Environmental / Sustainability status badges (e.g., "Cradle to Cradle Gold", "EPD Verified") use a tinted plaster background (`#F4F1EA`) with an understated eucalyptus hairline border (`#384637`) and 11px uppercase monospace-styled text.

### Material Cards
- Built on a `#F4F1EA` background bounded by a 1px `#E2DDD4` hairline border.
- Image containers maintain a strict 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratio with no corner rounding. Material photography highlights macro textures and grazing light.
- Content zone inside the card uses `space-md` internal padding, displaying the material code in `label-sm` slate gray above the product title in `headline-sm` serif.

### Input Fields
- Flat container with a 1px solid bottom or full perimeter border in `#D5CFBF`.
- Background rests at `#FAF8F5` or `#FFFFFF`.
- Focused state replaces the neutral border with an architectural charcoal line (`#1C1D1B`) at 1px thickness. Focus rings are disallowed.
- Error state replaces the border with terracotta clay (`#B3634B`). Helper text renders in `body-sm` terracotta.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp 16x16px squares with 0px radius and a 1px solid border in `#656763`.
- Checked state fills the box with `#384637` containing an unembellished geometric checkmark in `#FAF8F5`.
- Radio controls use concentric squares rather than circles to maintain the tectonic, orthogonal design identity.

### Lists & Data Matrices
- Technical specification lists feature hairline bottom borders (`#E2DDD4`) between rows.
- Left column (Attribute) renders in `body-sm` slate gray (`#656763`); right column (Value/Metric) renders in `body-md` obsidian (`#1C1D1B`) with aligned baseline numbers.
- Hovering over a list row reveals an understated surface wash of `#F4F1EA`.

### Architectural Spec Drawer (Specialized Component)
- A slide-over panel for BIM files, CAD details, and embodied carbon breakdowns.
- Pure planar `#FAF8F5` surface enclosed by a 1px `#D5CFBF` vertical division border, utilizing Newsreader headlines paired with high-density tabular sans layouts.
