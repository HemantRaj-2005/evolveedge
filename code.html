Accountability

Built with responsibility in mind.

Circular design is not an afterthought; it is our primary engineering requirement. We subject all material lines to lifecycle carbon assessments.

1. Responsible Sourcing

100% verified traceability back to regenerative forestry (FSC/PEFC) and single-origin regional European limestone quarries within 300km of our production nodes.

2. Low-Carbon Production

Ambient-temperature geopolymer curing eliminates traditional rotary kilns, achieving a verified -58% average embodied carbon footprint compared with Portland standards.

3. Durability & Resilience

Engineered for a century of service life. High flexural modulus and natural weathering patinas mitigate routine maintenance and chemical biocides.

4. High Recycled Content

Up to 88% verified post-industrial feedstocks, repurposing porcelain offcuts, slag vitrification, and mineral quarry tailings previously bound for landfills.

5. Circular End-of-Life

Dry-joint anchoring mechanics and bio-compatible matrices ensure 100% mechanical recyclability into sub-base aggregates or biological reintegration.

6. Rigorous Verification

Third-party certified ISO 14025 Environmental Product Declarations (Type III EPDs) independently audited by IBU (Institut Bauen und Umwelt e.V.).

Provenance & Process

From raw material to remarkable architecture.

Our process begins inside historic quarry beds and industrial processing centers throughout the Alpine and Nordic corridors. By reclaiming discarded sediment, fine stone slimes, and inert tailings, we reformulate stone with nature-mimicking mineral carbonation.

The result is a surface with the depth, nuance, and durability of millennia-old sedimentary stone, produced with an energy fraction of traditional masonry.
